How practice trades work

  1. You choose the share and the amount. It is bought as whole shares at the next market opening price (9:15 am on a market day).
  2. It is sold automatically if the price falls to its {{ term("stop", "safety level") }}, or when its {{ term("window", "holding window") }} ends. You can also press "Sell now", which sells at the next market opening price.
  3. Every trade pays real {{ term("charges", "charges") }}, and profits pay {{ term("tax", "short-term gains tax") }}, so the result is what you would really keep.
  4. Prices update once a day, around 7 pm, after the market closes.

To see how the app's own picks are doing, open the App's track record.
